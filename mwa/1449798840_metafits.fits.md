Although it sits in mwalib's `_bf` directory, its mode is `MODE = 'MWAX_CORRELATOR'`: a correlator observation that also carries a VOLTAGEBEAMS table of 9 coherent beams (`beam_index` 0–8, 6400 Hz resolution, `data_file_type = 19`, polarisation `XY`, creator `andrew`). 256 tiles (`NINPUTS = 512`), 80 kHz fine channels, metafits version 2.4, regenerated on 2026-01-06.
