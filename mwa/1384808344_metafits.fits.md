**Description**: Full, unmodified metafits for MWAX correlator observation 1384808344 (`MODE = 'MWAX_CORRELATOR'`, `FILENAME = 'Cal_HydA_Ch169'`, 2023-11-23T20:58:46, 56 s), a calibrator observation of Hydra A (`CALIBRAT = T`, `DELAYMOD = 'FULLTRACK'`), metafits version 2.4. It has 145 tiles (`NINPUTS = 290`): 112 `Tile…`, 72 `HexS…` and 72 `HexE…` inputs on `RRI` receivers, 32 long-baseline `LBF…`/`LBG…` inputs on `NI` receivers, and the two `CRAM` inputs on a `PSEUDO` receiver. Six cable flavours occur (`RG6_90`, `RG6_150`, `RG6_230`, `LMR400_320`, `RFOF-NI`, `CRAM`); 20 inputs are flagged.

TILEDATA has `Receiver_Types` but no `Whitening_Filter` column. mwalib reads `Whitening_Filter` per row and, when the lookup fails, falls back to deciding the filter from the flavour; its test expects `RG6_150` inputs to have the filter and `RG6_90` and `RFOF-NI` inputs not to.
